Welcome to my brand new developer blog! 

As a Full-Stack Engineer, I have always believed that owning your content and controlling your platform is one of the most important things you can do for your personal brand.

### Why Next.js?
I chose Next.js because of its incredible developer experience and its ability to effortlessly generate static HTML exports. This means my blog has **zero hosting costs**, loads instantly, and is incredibly SEO friendly.

### The Tech Stack
- **Next.js (App Router)** for routing and static generation
- **Tailwind CSS v4** for styling
- **GitHub Pages** for free, reliable hosting
- **Supabase** for dynamic comments (coming soon!)
